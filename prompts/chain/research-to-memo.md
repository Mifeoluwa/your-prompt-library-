# Research to Memo Prompt Chain

## Step 1
Summarize the research material.

## Step 2
Identify the three most important findings.

## Step 3
Write a professional business memo based on those findings.

## Constraints
- Do not invent facts.
- If evidence is insufficient, clearly state that.
- Keep the memo under 400 words.

## Output Format

1. Research Summary
2. Key Findings
3. Business Memo
